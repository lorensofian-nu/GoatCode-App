# Learning experience procedure

1. Define prerequisite knowledge and observable learning outcome.
2. Introduce the concept with intuitive language and a concrete analogy when useful.
3. Show a small worked example and explain each meaningful step.
4. Move to guided practice, then independent practice.
5. Design graduated hints and diagnose common misconceptions.
6. Give feedback that explains why, not just correct/incorrect.
7. Define evidence of mastery and a meaningful retry/review path.
8. Check cognitive load, inclusivity, accessibility and content accuracy.
9. Store lesson content in structured models rather than giant widget literals.
